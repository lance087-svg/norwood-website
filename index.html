Frequently Asked

Quick answers.

Do you have other styles and sizes of doors?
Yes. We stock the most popular styles and have over 1,000 doors in inventory — many are the same designs in different sizes and swings. We can order most any residential door. Call us with your description, sizes, and swing, or text a picture to (904) 403-5261.
Are your doors prehung or slab?
Both. We stock plenty of prehung interior and exterior units, and we also carry slabs. Which one fits your situation depends on your existing jamb and frame — give us a call and we'll help figure it out.
Are your exterior doors Florida approval code compliant?
Yes — many of our exterior doors meet Florida approval codes and we can provide approval numbers on request. Some discounted and clearance doors don't include approval numbers; we'll always tell you which is which.
Do you deliver?
Yes — within 75 miles of Jacksonville. Local delivery starts at $50 and runs up to about $100 depending on zip code and order size. Standard delivery is 3–4 days from order date; special orders may take longer.
Do you install?
We don't install, but we can recommend trusted installers in the Jacksonville area.
Are your interior wood doors solid?
We sell engineered solid-core shaker doors with wood stiles and rails and an MDF panel. We've found them substantially superior to all-wood doors — fewer warping issues, smoother panels, easier to paint. Old-school all-wood doors had problems we don't want to pass on to customers.
What about exterior door materials?
99% of our exterior doors are fiberglass — better performance against Florida's heat, humidity, and storms than wood. We can order solid wood exterior doors; they cost considerably more but we're glad to accommodate.
Do you offer warranties?
New doors carry a 1-year warranty from purchase date. Clearance and scratch-and-dent doors are specially priced and sold as-is without warranty — that's reflected in the discount.
Do you sell impact-rated and 8-foot doors?
Impact-rated doors are special order — we can quote whatever specs you need. 8-foot doors run about 20% of our sales; we keep some in stock but typically order to job. The ones we have on hand are priced very aggressively.
Bulk discounts?
Yes — orders of 15+ doors qualify for bulk pricing. Call for specifics. We also work regularly with property managers, landlords, and remodel contractors on repeat-business pricing.
How do I place an order?
Easiest way is to call us at (904) 768-6818. Doors involve a lot of variables — handing, dimensions, jamb depth, threshold, glass options — and a 5-minute conversation gets you the right unit at the right price faster than any web form.
